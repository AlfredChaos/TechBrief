# AI Insights Bilingual Blog - Product Requirements Document

## Overview
A high-fidelity web platform that curates content from Anthropic and OpenAI, translates it into Chinese, and provides an elegant reading experience. Includes an email subscription system and a full admin suite.

## Visual Style: "Liquid Glass"
- Frosted glass surfaces with backdrop-filter blur.
- Dopamine gradient accents (saturated highlights, thin borders, button glows).
- Apple Human Interface inspired: spacious, clean, refined.

## Screen List

### Public Experience
1. **Homepage**: Hero with gradient bloom, glass nav, featured content grid (Anthropic/OpenAI badges), source/type filters.
2. **Article Detail**: Long-form reading layout, original/translation toggle, side-by-side view option, floating subscribe prompt.
3. **Subscription Flow**: Glass modal for email signup with success states.

### Admin Experience (Management Suite)
4. **Admin Dashboard**: Overview stats, workflow status, recent activity in glass cards.
5. **Subscribers List**: Searchable/sortable table of email subscribers.
6. **Content Management**: List of articles/videos with translation and publish status.
7. **Workflow Monitor**: Timeline/log of automated fetching and translation jobs with status charts.
8. **Manual Publish**: Form for adding content manually with source selection and translation fields.

## Interaction Specs
- Responsive: Desktop (Primary), Tablet, Mobile.
- Hover/Active/Loading/Success states included.
- Light mode (primary) and Dark mode.
